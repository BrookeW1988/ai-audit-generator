# Intake form: business audit

**What it's for:** the client fills it in before the paid session, so the session goes on watching the work, not on basic questions. Every question maps to a fact in `references/what-a-good-audit-needs.md` (the IDs are in the table at the bottom, which never goes to the client).

**How to use it:** paste the questions into Google Forms, Tally or Typeform, or send it as a doc. Put the link in your brand sheet as [INTAKE FORM LINK].

Copy from the line below to the end of the questions.

---

## Before our session: [CLIENT BUSINESS]

This takes about 15 minutes. Short answers are fine. Rough numbers are fine. "Not sure" is a useful answer too, so please don't skip a question just because you don't know exactly.

**The job**

1. Which piece of work should we look at? Describe it in a sentence, the way you'd explain it to a new team member. *(e.g. "Getting new patients booked in, with their paperwork done.")*
2. What starts it, and how do you know it's finished? *(e.g. "It starts when an enquiry comes in through the website. It's finished when they're booked and their forms are in.")*
3. Who does each part? Names or roles are fine. Who has the final say?
4. How many of these happen in a normal week or month? And in a busy one?
5. Roughly how long does one take, start to finish? Has anyone ever timed it?

**What gets in the way**

6. Where does it get stuck, go wrong, or come back to be redone? Tell me about the last time it happened.
7. What does that cost you? Time, money, unhappy customers, late nights: whatever's true.
8. What have you already tried to fix it, and what happened?

**Tools and information**

9. Which programs or apps does this work go through? *(e.g. Xero, Gmail, a Google Sheet, your booking system)*
10. Is there any information in this work you'd never want in an AI tool? Has anyone decided which AI tools are OK to use?
11. What must a person always check before it goes out?

**Looking ahead**

12. If this work took half the time, what would you do with the hours?
13. What would make this audit worth it for you?
14. Roughly what's an hour of your time worth to the business? Skip it if you'd rather not say.
15. Besides you, who would need to agree to any changes?
16. Anything else I should know before we meet?

Thanks! See you on the day.
[YOUR FIRST NAME]
