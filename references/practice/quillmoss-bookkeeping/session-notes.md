# Session notes: Quillmoss Bookkeeping

Made up for practice. Numbered so the findings can point back to them.

## N01 — What they say they do
Dana: "Every month we send each client a report: their numbers and a few lines on what it means." She estimates "about 30 clients" get one each month.

## N02 — The steps, last month
Mia walked through last month. After month end she reconciles each client in Xero. She exports the profit and loss and the cash summary into the report template (a Google Doc), writes a commentary paragraph, and moves the Doc into Dana's "To review" folder. Dana reviews it, edits or rewrites the commentary, saves it as a PDF and emails it to the client.

## N03 — Shown on screen
Mia shared her screen and opened last month's report for one client, a café. It has a numbers table, Mia's commentary, and Dana's tracked changes. Dana's changes: she cut the technical wording, added a line telling the café owner to move money into their tax account, and deleted a sentence about a one-off expense. No copy of the report was handed over.

## N04 — What breaks
Dana: "Reports go out somewhere between the 12th and the 20th." She says clients ask "where's my report?" "a few times a month". She says reviewing takes her "most of two days" each month. Nobody has timed it.

## N05 — The rewrite loop
Mia: "I don't really know what Dana wants, so I guess." Dana: "It's quicker if I just rewrite it." There's no written guide to what a good commentary says. Sol doesn't write commentary. His reports go to Dana with the commentary left blank.

## N06 — Tools and data
Xero for the numbers, the Google Docs report template, Gmail, and a "Month-end" Google Sheet with client names and a tick when each report is sent. The Sheet was mentioned, not shown. Dana: "I'm not putting client financials into ChatGPT." Nobody has decided which AI tools, if any, are OK for client data.

## N07 — The request
Dana asked whether AI could "just write the reports". You said AI could help draft the commentary, but the bigger gap looked like the missing guide to what Dana wants, and that writing it down first would make any AI draft better. Dana wants to keep final sign-off on every report. Nothing was agreed.
