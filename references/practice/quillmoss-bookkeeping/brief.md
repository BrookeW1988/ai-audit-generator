# Quillmoss Bookkeeping

Practice client. Every person, business and detail here is made up.

- **Who:** Dana, the owner and registered BAS agent. Mia, bookkeeper. Sol, part-time bookkeeper.
- **What they asked:** "Can AI just write our monthly client reports?"
- **What you know going in:** they send each client a monthly report with their numbers from Xero and a short commentary on what happened that month.
- **This call:** the paid 90-minute working session. You asked them to show you how one report gets made, start to finish.
- **Before the session:** Dana filled in the intake form (`intake-answers.md`).
- **What you've been given:** your notes from the session in `session-notes.md` (already numbered). No reports, spreadsheets or templates have been handed over.
- **Who checks the findings and the report:** you. Dana reads the report and agrees to any work separately, on the walkthrough call.

## If the skill asks you something the call didn't cover

- What's an hour of Dana's time worth? "She didn't say." (So the numbers page shows hours, not dollars.)
- How long does one report take Mia? "Not timed. Nobody knows." (So it's To check.)
